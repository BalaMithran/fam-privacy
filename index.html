Privacy Policy

App: fam — Share your activity status with loved ones in real time
Developer: Mithran Bala
Last updated: 8 Nov 2026

1. Introduction

fam ("we", "our", "the app") is a private app for sharing what you're up to with the people you're close to. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why, how it is shared and stored, and how you can delete it. Using fam is also covered by our Terms of Use.

2. Information We Collect

Account information

  • Email address (required to create an account and sign in). Your password is handled by Firebase Authentication; we never see it.
  • Nickname and bio (set by you)
  • Profile photo and photos you post to your profile (optional, uploaded by you)
  • Your invite code, and the people you connect with, block or have pending requests with

Statuses and activity

  • The status text and emoji you set (e.g. "Working", "At the gym"), when you set it, and when it expires
  • Reactions to statuses, likes on photos, and "what are you up to?" requests you send or answer

Messages

  • Text messages, photos, videos and voice messages you send to your connections, and reactions to them
  • A text transcript of each voice message. The transcript is made on your phone by its on-device speech recognition; the recording is not sent anywhere for transcription. Phones without on-device recognition send the voice message without a transcript.
  • If you take a screenshot of a photo someone sent you, the sender is told.

Camera and microphone

  • The camera is used only when you take a photo or video to send or post.
  • The microphone is used only while you record a voice message, or when you speak a status into the home-screen widget. Spoken widget statuses are turned into text by your phone's speech recognition service (for example Google's on Android); fam receives only the resulting text.

Auto status (optional, off until you turn it on)

  • Android: "On the road" uses Android's physical activity recognition to tell when you're in a vehicle. "Asleep" uses your bedtime window, whether the phone is charging and whether it's idle. These signals are processed on your phone; only the resulting status (e.g. "On the road") is shared.
  • iPhone: a Focus filter you set up in iOS Settings tells fam which status to show while that Focus is on.
  • fam does not collect your location.

People you invite

  • If you invite or look up someone by email address, we store that address so the two of you are connected when they sign up. We don't email them; you share the invite yourself.

Device and usage data

  • Push notification tokens (Firebase Cloud Messaging, Apple Push Notification service, or Huawei Push Kit on Huawei phones), so we can deliver notifications
  • Your time zone offset, so reminders don't arrive at night
  • Crash reports: device model, OS version, app version and error details (Firebase Crashlytics)
  • Usage events such as screens opened and features used (Google Analytics for Firebase), linked to an app-instance identifier, not to your name or email. fam does not use the advertising ID.
  • An app integrity check (Firebase App Check with Google Play Integrity / Apple App Attest) that confirms requests come from the real app

Stored on your phone

  • Recent chat messages are cached on your phone so chats open quickly, and home-screen widgets keep your and your connections' latest statuses. Both are cleared when you sign out.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To run the app: share your status with your connections, deliver messages, keep widgets up to date, and set auto statuses you turned on
  • To send notifications: status updates, messages, reactions, connection requests, and reminders to update your status
  • To keep fam safe: review reports, remove content that breaks our Terms of Use, and block abuse
  • To find and fix crashes, and to understand which features are used so we can improve them

We do not sell your data, show ads, or use your data for advertising.

4. How We Share Your Data

  • With your connections: your nickname, bio, photos, status and the messages you send them are shared only with the people you have connected with. You choose who you connect with, and you can remove or block anyone at any time.
  • With Google (Firebase): fam runs on Google Firebase. Your data is stored and processed by Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Storage, Cloud Functions, Cloud Messaging, Crashlytics, Analytics and App Check. See Google's Privacy Policy and Firebase privacy.
  • With Huawei: on Huawei phones without Google services, notifications (including the text shown in them) are delivered through Huawei Push Kit. See Huawei's Privacy Statement.
  • With Apple: on iPhone, notifications are delivered through the Apple Push Notification service.
  • When the law requires it, or to protect someone's safety, including reporting child sexual abuse material to the authorities.

5. Data Retention

  • Your account, profile, saved statuses, activity history and connections are kept while your account exists.
  • Messages (text, photos, videos and voice) are deleted from our servers 24 hours after they're sent. A photo, video or voice message can be deleted sooner, once it has been opened. Your phone and your connection's phone may keep the chat in their local cache until you sign out.
  • If you report someone, a copy of the reported message and its media is kept so we can review it, even if either account is later deleted.
  • Crash reports and analytics are kept by Google under Firebase's retention settings (crash reports for up to 90 days).

6. Deleting Your Account

In the app: go to your profile, tap ⚙ Settings, then Delete Account. Deletion starts right away and removes your account, profile, photos, statuses, messages, connections, requests, reactions, blocks, invite code and notification tokens within a few minutes. Reports (see above) are kept.

Can't open the app? See fam-by-mj.web.app/delete-account or email us from the address you signed up with, and we'll delete it within 7 days.

7. Your Rights

You can see and edit your profile and statuses in the app, and delete your account at any time. You may also email us to access, correct or delete your personal data; we will respond within 30 days.

8. Children's Privacy

fam is not directed at children under 13, and you must be at least 13 to use it. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Security

All data is sent over HTTPS and encrypted at rest by Google. Access rules on our database and storage let only you and your connections read what is shared with them. We, the developer, can access data only to run the service and to review reports.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. If we make significant changes, we will tell you in the app. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
