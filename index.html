Privacy Policy

App: fam — Share your activity status with loved ones in real time
Last updated: 28 May 2025

1. Introduction

fam ("we", "our", "the app") is a private family sharing app. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why, and how it is stored and protected. By using fam you agree to this policy.

2. Information We Collect

Account information

  • Email address (required to create an account and sign in)
  • Display name / nickname (optional, set by you)
  • Profile photo (optional, uploaded by you)

Location data

  • Precise GPS coordinates of locations you designate as "home" or "work"
  • Background location access — only if you enable the geofence feature — to detect when you arrive at or leave your saved locations
  • Your current location label (e.g. "at home", "at work") is shared in real time with your connected family members

Photos and media

  • Photos you send as snaps are uploaded to our cloud storage
  • Your profile photo is uploaded to our cloud storage
  • Photos received from family members may be saved to your device photo library if you choose to save them

Status and activity data

  • Status text and emoji you set (e.g. "Working", "Cooking")
  • Timestamps of status updates
  • Reactions you send to family members' statuses

Device and usage data

  • Crash reports — device model, OS version, app version, and error stack traces, collected automatically to help us fix bugs
  • Anonymous usage events (e.g. screens visited, features used) — no personally identifiable information is included in analytics
  • Push notification token (to deliver status and snap notifications to your device)

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide the app's core features: real-time status sharing, snap photo messaging, and home screen widgets
  • To send push notifications when a family member updates their status or sends you a snap
  • To trigger automatic status updates when you arrive at or leave a saved location (geofence feature, opt-in only)
  • To diagnose and fix app crashes and errors
  • To understand how features are used so we can improve the app

4. How We Share Your Data

We do not sell your data. Data is shared only in these cases:

  • With your family connections: your status, location label, profile photo, and snaps are shared with the specific people you have connected with in the app. You control who you connect with.
  • With Google / Firebase: the app is built on Google Firebase. Your data is stored in Firebase Cloud Firestore (database), Firebase Storage (photos), Firebase Authentication (login), and Google's servers. Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics also send data to Google. See Google's Privacy Policy.

5. Data Retention

  • Your data is retained for as long as your account is active
  • Snaps are stored until both sender and receiver have viewed them, after which they may be deleted from storage
  • To delete your account and all associated data, contact us at the email below

6. Your Rights

You may request to access, correct, or delete your personal data at any time by contacting us. We will respond within 30 days.

7. Children's Privacy

fam is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.

8. Security

All data is transmitted over HTTPS. Firebase applies encryption at rest and in transit. Access to your data is restricted to your connected family members and to us for the purpose of operating the service.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. If we make significant changes, we will notify you through the app. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance.
